# Brand assets

Two marks, one card. The app icon and the illustration share their geometry —
Argon's Polaroid with a coral pin breaking out of the top-right corner — and
differ only in what the photograph inside the card shows.

| file | where it is used |
|---|---|
| `app-icon.svg` | Windows taskbar, Start menu, `.ico`, Android launcher, splash |
| `about-icon.svg` | the About pane's illustration (Settings → App) |

## Where they came from

The card, the back card's amber photo showing at the edges, the caption strip,
the amber tag and the pin are Argon's Concept D, taken number for number from
`reference/render_logos_v2.py`. The photograph inside the card is the owner's
own frame of Beachy Head; the app icon keeps a single teal hill and the Naze
Tower, the About illustration keeps the full coastline.

Rules that were paid for in iterations, and that any change here has to keep:

- The chalk/landscape is **not** a gradient over its whole face; a single
  vertical gradient reads as cloth.
- Small-size marks are **not** downscales: at 32px and below the card's margin
  and the tower collapse. A pixel-grid pass is drawn separately if one is needed
  (`make-small-marks.py`, kept for reference; currently unused — 48px still reads
  from the master).
- The pin closes to a **point** at y=45; its sides leave the head at the tangent
  points (`acos(12.5/22.5)`) and bow inward. Straight sides read as a triangle
  glued to a circle.
- The tower is the Naze Tower: octagonal, brick, crenellated, **no lantern
  room, no flag**.
- The land leans green (`#129C9B → #045057`), not blue: it sits between the cyan
  sky and the brick tower.

## Regenerating

```
python docs/brand/make-app-icon.py     # rewrites app-icon.svg
node   docs/brand/make-icons.mjs       # desktop/build/icon.png + .ico, app/public/brand/*
node   docs/brand/make-android-icons.mjs  # the five mipmap buckets + adaptive preview
```

`about-icon.svg` is not regenerated by a script: it was produced once by
patching the coastal landscape into Argon's frame, and its generator
(`reference/build-about-icon.py`) depends on a file that is no longer kept.
Edit the SVG directly.

## Checking a change

Rasterise at the size it ships at and look at it — 256 for About, 48/32 for the
launcher, 16 for the title bar:

```
node -e "const s=require('sharp');(async()=>{const b=await s('docs/brand/app-icon.svg',{density:144}).png().toBuffer();for(const n of [256,48,32,16])await s(b).resize(n,n).png().toFile('docs/brand/check-'+n+'.png')})()"
```

`reference/` holds Argon's original renders, the concept sheet the direction was
chosen from, and his generator scripts. Nothing there is used at build time.
